Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the independent information website Printer Resolve, published from Kashmir, India, handles information. For questions or privacy requests, email support@printerresolve.com.

Information you choose to send

The site has no support request form, account registration or paid checkout. If you contact us by email, we receive the address, message and attachments you send. We use these to respond to website enquiries, review editorial corrections, and handle privacy or legal requests. We do not sell email correspondence.

Do not send passwords, one-time codes, identity documents, full card numbers or bank details. This site does not need these to provide free guides.

Hosting and email providers

The website is hosted using Cloudflare Pages and uses Zoho for business email. These services may process technical data such as IP addresses, browser information, timestamps, security events and message-delivery information. See the Cloudflare privacy policy and Zoho privacy policy.

Advertising, Google AdSense and tracking technologies

Printer Resolve has included Google AdSense verification/advertising code while applying to show ads. Approval is not guaranteed, and the presence of the code does not mean advertising is currently active. When Google ad technologies are used, Google and other third-party advertising vendors may place or read cookies, use web beacons, IP addresses, device identifiers or similar technologies to serve ads, limit repeated advertising, measure effectiveness, help detect invalid traffic and, where permitted, personalize ads.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to show advertisements based on a visitor's earlier visits to Printer Resolve or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this website and/or other websites on the internet. You can manage Google's personalized advertising in Google My Ad Center / Ads Settings. You can also learn about opt-outs offered by participating third parties at AboutAds.info. Depending on your location and browser, opting out of personalization may not prevent all advertisements or all essential fraud-prevention storage.

For more information, read How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, Google advertising technologies, and Google's publisher privacy disclosures. The specific third-party advertising providers used can depend on Google's publisher settings and the advertising partners enabled.

Consent and regional choices

Some regions, including the European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland, require additional disclosures and consent choices. When personalized Google advertising is served to visitors in these regions, Google requires publishers to use a Google-certified consent management platform that integrates with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework. A written privacy policy does not substitute for a functioning consent platform. We will need to verify the applicable consent message is correctly configured and operating before enabling the corresponding advertising features for those visitors.

If you do not see consent controls, that absence should not be interpreted as consent. You may also adjust your browser's cookie controls or use Google's advertising controls linked above. To ask about consent choices or your personal information, contact support@printerresolve.com.

Analytics and local diagnostic tools

Our problem checker, paper-fit tool, connection-range calculator and troubleshooting-note generator process entered diagnostic information locally in your browser; the tools do not upload entries to our servers. Ordinary hosting requests can still generate security and access logs. If analytics scripts or advertising measurement tools are configured in the future, this policy must be reviewed to describe their actual operation and any consent choices. We do not claim these local tools can scan a device or confirm printer connectivity.

Safety is part of useful guidance.

Examples and diagrams are illustrations unless a real test is explicitly documented. Your device remains under your control.

Data storage and sharing

We keep correspondence only as reasonably needed for the enquiry, legal duties, abuse prevention or dispute resolution. Service providers may retain operational logs and backups according to their policies. Information may be disclosed where legally required or needed to defend a legitimate claim. Data may be processed in countries where our providers operate.

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct or delete personal information associated with your enquiry or raise a privacy concern by writing to support@printerresolve.com with the subject “Privacy request.” We may need reasonable verification, and certain records may need to be retained by law.

Policy updates

If practices change, we will update this page and its effective date. Please check this notice periodically.
